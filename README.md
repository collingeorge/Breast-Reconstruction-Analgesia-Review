## Overview

This repository contains a narrative literature review of perioperative analgesia in breast reconstruction surgery, with an evidence-gap analysis for breast implant revision, plus three companion narrative reviews that examine the same setting from distinct analytical perspectives: analgesic strategy, patient communication, and recovery measurement.

All documents are narrative literature reviews prepared as part of an independent academic portfolio project. The main manuscript documents its PubMed search strategy, but it is not a systematic review or meta-analysis. None of the documents constitute clinical protocols, institutional guidelines, order sets, or decision-support tools.

## Intellectual Architecture

The three companion documents examine distinct but related dimensions of the same perioperative setting:

- **02** addresses what the published literature says about analgesic strategy and its measured outcomes
- **03** addresses how communication quality shapes whether patients can meaningfully participate in those decisions
- **04** addresses whether the outcome constructs used to evaluate analgesic strategies adequately capture patient-experienced recovery

The main manuscript brings these together with two further domains (regional techniques and adjuvant pharmacology) and an evidence-gap analysis for implant revision.

## Interpretive Caution

The literature reviewed in these documents spans heterogeneous clinical populations, institutional practices, anesthetic techniques, and evidence quality levels. All documents are narrative literature reviews. No PRISMA framework, formal risk-of-bias assessment, GRADE rating, or data pooling was applied. Evidence strength is described only by study design (for example, "meta-analysis of RCTs").

Findings discussed across these documents reflect the published literature and should not be read as validated clinical guidance, performance targets, or prescriptive standards of any kind.

## Evidence Base

The documents draw on published literature including the ERAS Society consensus review for breast reconstruction, the PROSPECT guideline for oncological breast surgery, the SAMBA practice advisory on pectoralis blocks, ASRA Pain Medicine guidance on local anesthetic systemic toxicity and antithrombotic therapy, consensus PONV guidelines, randomized trials and meta-analyses on regional techniques and opioid-sparing adjuncts, health literacy and shared decision-making research, and perioperative recovery measurement research.

Reference identifiers are provided for audit; this release-preparation pass does not establish that every citation and supporting claim has been re-verified. Full references with PMIDs and DOIs are provided in [`06_References.md`](06_References.md).

## Source/version update — 27 October 2026

The 2020 PONV consensus and 2018 antithrombotic guideline are historical sources. A [fifth PONV consensus executive summary](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41237407/) and [ASRA fifth-edition guideline](https://asra.com/guidelines-articles/guidelines) are now available. Their existence does not validate this portfolio as an implementation guide. [Foo et al. 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33141959/) provides specific IV-lidocaine safety cautions regarding other local-anesthetic interventions. Historical PROSPECT gabapentin recommendations should be read alongside [Verret et al. 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32667154/), rather than treated as an unconditional routine-use endorsement.

## Corrections and qualifications

The network meta-analysis's nonsignificant chronic-pain result is not proof that a technique has no effect. The review now uses detected-difference wording. The Chen trial's primary QoR finding is not an equivalence result or proof of an instrument-sensitivity problem.

The PONV fourth consensus and ASRA antithrombotic fourth edition are historical; newer fifth editions are linked in the documents. IV lidocaine plus regional local anesthetics is not justified merely by a cumulative-dose calculation. A historical procedure-specific gabapentin recommendation does not erase later mixed-surgery benefit/harm evidence.

An apparently valid DOI does not authenticate every sentence citing it. Exact statistical extraction, full-text risk of bias, supplements, current product labeling, corrections/retractions, all search results, rights and external deposits have not received exhaustive review. The failed/partial historical queries do not support a claim that revision-specific research is absent.
